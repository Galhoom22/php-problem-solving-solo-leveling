## 16. Decision Summary

The current repo works because it is:
1. **Dedicated to one thing**
2. **Ordered as a three-stage path**
3. **Broken into small completable sessions**
4. **Visibly progressive**
5. **Grounded in official sources**
6. **Student-driven (hands-on writing)**
7. **Protected by reviews and milestones against false mastery**

The new Problem Solving repo should keep all of that, and add:
8. **Solo Leveling System** for fun addiction
9. **Massive PHP-only multi-platform problem bank**
10. **Strict Difficulty Progression Law**
11. **Learn + Solve for PHP Problem Solving + DSA** — so a total beginner can enter, understand Gate by Gate, and become dramatically stronger as Rank rises
12. **FAANG-transferable interview strength** — honest that PHP is not the most common FAANG coding language, but patterns + explanation still prepare Big Tech rounds; polyglot hunters can transfer later
13. **Backend career links** — every major pattern maps to direct/indirect backend job value so hunters know why they are training
14. **PHP Fitness Filter** — required climb stays PHP-natural; no linked-list theater early
16. **Hunter solves first / no direct spoilers** (`21`) — problems authored without answers; AI briefs then coaches discovery

Transfer the structured learning system into a PHP 8.5 Problem Solving + DSA repo, and ship hunters who can clear large-company and FAANG-style coding interviews:

> Learn the Skill. Enter the Gate. Attempt the clear yourself.  
> Ask for hints — not cheats. Level Up. Arise.  
> Put on Interview Armor. Transfer your power. Walk into the interview ready.
